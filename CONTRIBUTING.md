# Contributing

Synthetic Arts Council is an experimental decision-support project. Contributions should strengthen traceability, uncertainty handling, cultural legitimacy, and human review rather than imply that model output has institutional authority.

## Before contributing

- Open an issue describing the proposed change and the problem it addresses.
- Do not submit private institutional records, personal data, confidential communications, or material without the right to share it.
- Do not claim to represent an institution, artistic discipline, community, or stakeholder without documented authority.
- Keep source material, extracted evidence, interpretation, recommendation, and verified outcome distinct.

## Evidence contributions

Every evidence item must reference a registered source and include its context, date, geography, reliability assessment, and limitations. Conflicting evidence should be preserved, not silently reconciled.

## Validation

Run:

```sh
ruby scripts/validate.rb
```

All automatic checks must pass. Human review is still required for whether evidence supports a claim and whether affected stakeholders are adequately represented.
