# Mandate

## Experimental mandate

Synthetic Arts Council tests whether discipline-specific AI committee members can use a shared evidence bundle to produce a transparent, traceable recommendation on a cultural-policy question.

It may:

- organize sources and evidence;
- produce independent, structured committee positions;
- identify agreement, dissent, uncertainty, evidence gaps, and missing stakeholders;
- prepare a draft recommendation and decision record for human review.

It may not:

- claim to be Dewan Kesenian Jakarta or any other institution;
- represent artists, communities, disciplines, or jurisdictions;
- replace public consultation, cultural representation, or institutional authority;
- convert a model output into an institutional decision;
- conceal uncertainty, dissent, absent stakeholders, or missing evidence.
