# Limitations

- Committee configurations do not confer cultural representation or lived experience.
- Model reasoning may be inconsistent, biased, overconfident, or sensitive to prompt and model changes.
- Shared evidence can still be incomplete, contested, outdated, or incorrectly extracted.
- A numeric confidence score is not calibrated truth unless a later evaluation demonstrates calibration.
- Majority direction is not automatically legitimate, equitable, or institutionally valid.
- Some questions require public consultation, domain experts, legal review, affected-community participation, or political accountability that the experiment cannot supply.
- The Jakarta reference is an adaptation aid, not permission to claim affiliation with or authority from Dewan Kesenian Jakarta.
- The archive statistic in the README is a research input whose source trail must be attached before external publication.
- v0.1 specifies contracts and governance constraints; it does not yet demonstrate that model outputs satisfy them.
