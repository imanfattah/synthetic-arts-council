# Methodology principles

## Source and evidence separation

A source is a complete record. Evidence is a bounded claim, provision, excerpt, or data point extracted from it. Each evidence item retains its source reference, context, date, geography, reliability assessment, and limitations.

## Independent first positions

Each committee receives the same case and evidence bundle and forms its initial position without access to other committees' answers. This reduces artificial consensus and makes convergence or disagreement inspectable.

## Structured deliberation

The orchestrator compares explicit fields. It must retain agreement, disagreement, cross-disciplinary effects, gaps, missing stakeholders, majority direction, and minority positions.

## Evidence gate

When relevant evidence is insufficient, the valid output is a request for evidence or consultation—not a forced recommendation.

## Human authority

Recommendations remain drafts until reviewed through a named human and institutional process. The simulation never creates its own authority.
